Company: Example Co
Title: Solutions Engineer

About the role
Example Co builds developer tools for software teams. As a Solutions Engineer, you'll partner with our sales team to help prospects understand, evaluate, and adopt the product.

What you'll do
- Lead technical product demos and discovery calls with prospective customers.
- Run proofs of concept: define success criteria, troubleshoot integrations, and get customers to a decision.
- Write scripts and sample code that show customers how to integrate the product with their existing systems.
- Share customer feedback and feature requests with Product and Engineering.
- Create technical documentation and guides for common use cases.
- Work with Customer Success to support renewals and expansion.

What we're looking for
- 2+ years in a customer-facing technical role such as solutions engineering, technical support, or consulting.
- Ability to read and write scripts in Python or JavaScript.
- Experience working with REST APIs and webhooks.
- Familiarity with Git and source control workflows.
- Strong written and verbal communication skills.
- Willingness to travel up to 20%.

Nice to have
- Experience with cloud platforms such as AWS or GCP.
- Background in data engineering or analytics tools.
- Experience selling to enterprise customers.
